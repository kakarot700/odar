# Research Report: Does raising the minimum wage reduce employment? Summarize what the empirical evidence says, including where economists disagree.

## Answer (written only from the certified findings below)
### Summary
The magnitude of any employment effect depends on the elasticity of labor demand rather than the wage floor alone [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws). When demand is less elastic, employment typically changes less after a wage increase [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws). President Ronald Reagan and conservative economists have argued that minimum wages hurt the very workers they were meant to help [[4]](https://govfacts.org/rights-freedoms/economic-workplace-rights/minimum-wage-overtime/debate-increasing-the-minimum-wage/). A novel bunching‑based method estimates employment effects by comparing excess jobs at or slightly above the new minimum with missing jobs paying below it [[2]](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25434).

### What are minimum wage laws, key definitions, and why does the employment effect matter economically?
Like the federal wage and hour law, state law often exempts particular occupations or industries from the minimum labor standard generally applied to covered employment [[1]](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/mw-consolidated). For example, the minimum wage for employers who employ fewer than six people and employees engaged in seasonal employment in New Jersey is $15.23 per hour [[1]](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/mw-consolidated).

The size of the employment effect depends on the elasticity of labor demand, not just on the wage floor itself [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws). If labor demand is elastic, a higher minimum wage is more likely to reduce employment because firms can cut back on workers more easily [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws). In a competitive labor market, a higher minimum wage can reduce employment if firms react by hiring fewer workers [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws).

### What does the main empirical evidence say about how minimum wage increases affect employment, and which studies anchor that view?
Some studies report minimal employment impact when adjustments are modest, whereas others identify clear trade‑offs in which higher wages are accompanied by lower employment levels [[4]](https://govfacts.org/rights-freedoms/economic-workplace-rights/minimum-wage-overtime/debate-increasing-the-minimum-wage/).

### Where do economists disagree about minimum wage effects, and what are the main methodological limitations and alternative mechanisms?
This claim frames the disagreement as a trade-off between intended benefits and unintended harm, since the workers said to lose out are precisely the group the policy is designed to serve [[4]](https://govfacts.org/rights-freedoms/economic-workplace-rights/minimum-wage-overtime/debate-increasing-the-minimum-wage/).

### What are the latest developments, including recent research and policy changes, on minimum wage and employment?
These studies examine how a wage floor changes wages, employment, and efficiency in different labor markets, an approach used in Principles of Economics [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws). The direction of employment effects is conditioned by elasticity: if demand is less elastic, employment usually changes less when the wage floor rises [[3]](https://fiveable.me/principles-econ/key-terms/minimum-wage-laws).
