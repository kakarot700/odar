Answer (verbatim):
Usually no. The best evidence says intermittent fasting and daily calorie restriction produce similar weight loss overall when total calories are similar. Some specific fasting styles, like alternate-day fasting, can sometimes show a small edge in certain studies, but it's usually modest and not consistent.
