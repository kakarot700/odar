In phase 3 trials, R21/Matrix-M showed about 75% efficacy against symptomatic malaria in children when given before the high-transmission season, and about 68% efficacy in perennial transmission settings over the first 12 months after the primary 3-dose series. In the Burkina Faso phase 2b trial, it also met the WHO target of 75% efficacy in young children over one year, with a booster helping maintain protection.
