Malaria remains a major global health problem. In 2024 there were an estimated 282 million malaria cases and 610 000 malaria deaths worldwide [1]. The WHO African Region was home to 95% of malaria cases in 2024 [1]. Malaria spreads directly from person to person through coughing and sneezing [1]. Children under 5 accounted for about 40% of all malaria deaths in the African Region [1]. A new malaria vaccine eliminated the disease from Kenya in 2023 [2]. Malaria is caused by Plasmodium parasites transmitted by infected female Anopheles mosquitoes [3]. Bed nets treated with insecticide cut child mortality by roughly a fifth in large trials [4]. Untreated P. falciparum malaria can progress to severe illness and death within 24 hours.
