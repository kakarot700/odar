# Research Report: What were the main outcomes and agreements of the COP30 UN climate summit held in Belem, Brazil in November 2025?

## Answer (written only from the certified findings below)
The COP30 climate summit, which brought the world to Belém, Brazil in November 2025, launched a voluntary plan to curb fossil fuels, set a goal to triple adaptation finance, and put forward new efforts to "strengthen" climate targets [[1]](https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop30-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-belem)[[4]](https://unu.edu/ehs/article/5-outcomes-cop-30-what-belem-political-package-really-delivered). Together, these outcomes advance the implementation of the Paris Agreement and provide guidance for international cooperation on climate action [[3]](https://cop30.br/en/cop30-outcomes). The summit aimed to establish clear pathways to deliver past pledges and put the world on a safer track, at a time when efforts to halt temperature rise are severely off track and climate disasters are becoming ever-more destructive [[2]](https://www.wri.org/insights/cop30-outcomes-next-steps).

One of the possible overarching outcomes was a so-called "mutirão decision," widely interpreted as a text containing the key agreements from COP30 [[1]](https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop30-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-belem), and one of the biggest negotiated outcomes concerned efforts to adapt to the impacts of climate change, which Corrêa do Lago dubbed the "COP of adaptation" [[1]](https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop30-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-belem). Trade also featured in the new Integrated Forum on Climate Change and Trade, introduced by President Lula during the World Leaders' Summit held just before COP30 [[2]](https://www.wri.org/insights/cop30-outcomes-next-steps). WRI experts have set out to unpack what the 2025 UN climate summit achieved, where it fell short, and what comes next, though the facts provided do not specify what those shortfalls were [[2]](https://www.wri.org/insights/cop30-outcomes-next-steps).
