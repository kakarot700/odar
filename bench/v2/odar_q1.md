# Research Report: What were the main outcomes and agreements of the COP30 UN climate summit held in Belem, Brazil in November 2025?

## Answer (written only from the certified findings below)
The 30th Conference of the Parties on Climate Change (COP30) took place in Belém, Brazil, from 10 to 21 November 2025, bringing together nearly 200 countries to discuss global climate policies [[2]](https://www.globalcompactnetwork.org/en/news/ungcn-italy-news/2639-cop30-in-belem-main-outcomes-and-climate-agreements.html). The summit aimed to establish clear pathways to deliver past climate pledges and put the world on a safer track, amid efforts to halt temperature rise being severely off track and climate disasters growing ever-more destructive [[3]](https://www.wri.org/insights/cop30-outcomes-next-steps).

Among the most significant negotiated outcomes was a commitment to triple funding for climate adaptation — to USD 1.3 trillion per year — by 2035, with a focus on the most vulnerable countries, and Corrêa do Lago dubbed COP30 the "COP of adaptation" in recognition of this emphasis [[2]](https://www.globalcompactnetwork.org/en/news/ungcn-italy-news/2639-cop30-in-belem-main-outcomes-and-climate-agreements.html)[[1]](https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop30-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-belem)[[2]](https://www.globalcompactnetwork.org/en/news/ungcn-italy-news/2639-cop30-in-belem-main-outcomes-and-climate-agreements.html). Alongside this, a voluntary plan to curb fossil fuels, a goal to triple adaptation finance, and new efforts to "strengthen" climate targets were launched at the summit [[1]](https://www.carbonbrief.org/cop30-key-outcomes-agreed-at-the-un-climate-talks-in-belem). In addition, the summit approved the Belém Health Action Plan (BHAP), which aims to strengthen health systems and make them more resilient to the effects of climate change [[2]](https://www.globalcompactnetwork.org/en/news/ungcn-italy-news/2639-cop30-in-belem-main-outcomes-and-climate-agreements.html).

Trade also featured in the summit's outcomes, with the new Integrated Forum on Climate Change and Trade introduced by President Lula during the World Leaders' Summit held just before COP30 [[3]](https://www.wri.org/insights/cop30-outcomes-next-steps). WRI experts have since unpacked what the 2025 UN climate summit achieved, where it fell short, and what comes next, suggesting that further details on remaining gaps and follow-up actions are available in their analysis rather than in the facts above [[3]](https://www.wri.org/insights/cop30-outcomes-next-steps).
